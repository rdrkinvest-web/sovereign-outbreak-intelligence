Every step

    This console shows only what the regional coordinator receives: the questions it sends, the names of the checks each node reports running, and aggregate answers. Individual check results and patient records never leave a national node, so they never reach this page. Local checks are drawn from each node's reported list, just before its answer. Replies to one question come back together, so their order on screen is illustrative. Synthetic demonstration data; confidence is a demo score.
